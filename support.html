Help with your little laptop

Your own little laptop, with a local library you can make yours.

Choose a laptop or interface

Pinch inward over the keyboard or display, or use the themes key. Choose a laptop to apply its paired interface. The interfaces tab lets you choose one independently. Tap the selected item or pinch outward to return.

Add and move stickers

Double-tap bare laptop material to browse stickers. Choose the smiling face for emoji and supported personal stickers or Genmoji. Photos, Files and Paste let you add your own images. Drag, pinch and twist placed stickers. Drag a selected sticker into the bin to remove it; use Undo to bring it back.

Genmoji or Paste is unavailable

Keyboard features depend on your device, language and Apple settings. Regular emoji and photo imports remain available. Paste needs a supported image on the clipboard; try Photos or Files if an app copies a link instead of image data. lil laptop only reads clipboard content after you choose Paste.

Save, share and protect your library

Customize contains Saved setups and Help & privacy. Documents belong to one shared local library. Default-private setup images hide documents and personal desktop objects, but deliberately placed photo stickers remain visible. Review the image before sharing. Setup images are pictures, not library backups.

Recovery and missing imports

If a save is unreadable, the app preserves recovery data and tries the previous snapshot. A missing imported file leaves its library entry available. Avoid deleting the app while troubleshooting: reinstalling can remove local notes and media. Check free storage and include the exact error when asking for help.

Compatibility and displays

The composition uses a fixed laptop posture. It contours the Duo display and fits the complete laptop against black on other screen shapes. The app does not promise an always-on or simultaneously active decorative lid.

Get help

Contact the publisher below with your app version, device model, OS version and steps to reproduce the issue. Please omit private notes, photos and other sensitive content. No password or account is needed to use lil laptop.
